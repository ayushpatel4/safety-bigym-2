

## Project Assessment
Minimum requirements to pass the project
The project pass mark for BEng undergraduate students is 40% and for MEng undergraduate
students is 50%.
To pass the project, you must:
- Submit an interim report, judged by your supervisor and second marker to be of an
acceptable standard.
- Submit a final report, judged by your supervisor and second marker to be of an acceptable
standard.
- Complete a project presentation at a scheduled date of time, judged by an assessment team
to be of an acceptable standard.
The overall level of project achievement (the final grade) is determined through academic
judgement by an assessment team with input from the project supervisor and second marker.
The marks awarded by individual assessment teams are further cross checked and moderated
to assure fairness and consistency of marking, with the process checked by external examiners.
All marks are awarded based on academic judgement.
MEng Project Assessment Criteria
MEng level projects are expected to be approached as research projects. This means that they
should include sound background research, a solid implementation or equivalent piece of
theoretical work, and a thorough evaluation of the project's output in both absolute and relative
terms. The very best MEng projects invariably cover some new ground, for example by
developing a complex application which does not already exist, or by enhancing some existing
application or method to improve its functionality, performance.
A straightforward implementation project is acceptable, but it is unlikely to gain high marks at
MEng level, regardless of how well it is done. Projects which are predominantly extended
literature reviews are not acceptable. These would need to be backed up by experimentation,
implementation, or theoretical analysis.
It can be valuable to publish a research paper alongside your project, or to present at a suitable
conference. This is not directly taken into account during assessment as not all students are

afforded this opportunity, but this can be a useful way for you to demonstrate that there is wider
interest in your work.

BEng Project Assessment Criteria
The assessment for BEng projects is largely equivalent to the MEng version, but this takes into
account the lower academic level of the project, that students have not studied as many
advanced modules or attended a formal placement, and that the weighting for BEng projects is
lower than it is for MEng projects.
BEng students can choose to work on a research focused project, but they can also instead opt
to focus on engineering a solid solution to a technical problem. BEng students are still expected
to incorporate relevant existing ideas, to refer to and build upon relevant academic research, and
to suitably evaluate the work they have completed.
